## Important checks when buying cross-border

- Confirm whether the advertised price includes VAT and whether VAT is reclaimable.
- Check the vehicle registration documents and EU type-approval status.
- Request a damp-test report, service history and accident history.
- Verify that the seller, chassis number and bank details are genuine.
- Check warranty coverage outside the seller’s country.
- Budget for transport, temporary plates, insurance, registration and taxes.
- Be especially careful with UK imports into the EU because customs and tax rules may apply.
